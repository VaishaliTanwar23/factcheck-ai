# 30-second demo video script

0–5 sec: Show the FactCheck AI homepage and say: “This is my PDF fact-checking application.”

5–10 sec: Upload `sample_trap_document.pdf`.

10–18 sec: Click “Extract claims and fact-check” and show the progress bar and summary counts.

18–25 sec: Open one claim result. Show the status, explanation and clickable web source links.

25–30 sec: Click “Download results as CSV” and show the downloaded report.

Recording tip: Use Windows Snipping Tool screen recording or Xbox Game Bar (Win + Alt + R). Keep the browser zoom at 90–100% and ensure the app URL is visible.
